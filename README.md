# Superstore Sales Dashboard – Key Insights

## 1. Sales Performance

* Total sales reached approximately **$2.29M** during the analyzed period.
* Highest sales were observed in **November and December**, indicating strong year-end performance.

## 2. Regional Analysis

* The **West region** contributed the highest share of total profit (~38%).
* The **Central region** showed the lowest profit contribution among all regions.

## 3. Product Category Insights

* **Phones** and **Chairs** were among the top-performing categories by sales.
* Some categories generated high sales but comparatively lower profit contribution.

## 4. State-wise Performance

* **California** recorded the highest sales among all states.
* Sales distribution varied significantly across states, highlighting regional demand differences.

## 5. Dashboard Features

Interactive slicers were implemented for:

* Region
* Category
* Month

KPI cards were added to monitor:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders

## Conclusion

This dashboard helps analyze sales performance, profit contribution, and regional trends efficiently through interactive reporting and visualization techniques in Microsoft Excel.
